Community Board: Garden, Food Trucks, Yard Sales & Farm

A place for neighbors to list extra garden veggies or eggs, a food truck stop or coffee special, a yard sale, or hay and farm goods. Garden and farm listings stay up for 30 days unless you give us an end date; coffee specials stay up for 7 days; yard sales and today’s food-truck posts come down after that day.

Bookmark this page and check back often! New listings go up daily. On a computer, press Ctrl+D (or Cmd+D on a Mac). On a phone, use Share > Add to Home Screen.

Food Trucks & Coffee

Food trucks can post today’s location and hours for free. Coffee shops and coffee huts can post a special for free. Weekly or monthly food-truck schedules, and ads for bigger restaurants, are paid listings. Food-truck schedules are $15/week or $30/month, and restaurant ads are $30/week or $100/month — see Pricing above.

Hay & Farm

Local listings

List it free

Garden veggies and eggs, yard sales, and hay or farm goods are free for local household and farm sellers — not commercial dealers. Food trucks can post today’s location and hours for free. Coffee shops and coffee huts can post a special for free. Tell us what you want listed, a price if you want it shown, your town or today’s spot, and how buyers can reach you. For a yard sale or a food-truck day post, include the date and times.

Weekly or monthly food-truck schedules, and ads for bigger restaurants, are paid listings. Food-truck schedules are $15/week or $30/month, and restaurant ads are $30/week or $100/month — see Pricing above.
