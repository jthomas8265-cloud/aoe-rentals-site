Rental Terms

Last updated September 23, 2026

These are the high-level terms for renting from Agent Orange Equipment Rentals LLC, doing business as AOE Rentals, 310 Coffeen St, Ranchester, WY 82839. They are not the full rental contract. The written rental agreement we send when you book is what controls that rental.

The rental

We rent a 2017 Kubota L-47. You tell us the place or job site. We schedule drop-off and later pick-up within about 25 miles of Ranchester, Wyoming. A week includes free local drop-off and pick-up. Daily jobs may have a drop-off and pick-up charge. Story and mountain runs are not available. Hours are Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, upon availability.

You operate

The customer is the operator. We drop the machine off and pick it up. We do not provide an operator. You are responsible for running it safely and for anyone you allow near the job.

Deposit

A refundable deposit hold is required before release. The amount is the deposit on our rate card and in the written agreement (currently $500). It comes back after a clean handback, less any charges the written agreement allows, such as damage, missing fuel, or hours past the included time.

Insurance

You provide proof of insurance before we release the machine, and you keep that coverage in place for the rental. You are responsible for the machine while it is in your care.

Rates

Daily, weekly, and overtime rates are the prices listed on aoerentalswyo.com unless we confirm a different price in writing. A booking is not final until we confirm the dates and you accept the written rental agreement.

Texts about your rental
