Sheridan Building Permit Leads

A monthly sheet of City of Sheridan building permits, sorted so the best work-finding leads come first. Built from the city’s free public permit reports — we just make them useful for the trades.

Starting at $20/month. Text to subscribe or ask questions. No signup form on this page — just call or text the business line below.

What’s in each sheet

Every month’s report includes

  • Permit date and type (new construction, alteration, addition, and so on)
  • Job address and a short description of what’s being built
  • Reported project cost
  • Owner or contractor of record
  • Likely trades needed (drywall, plumbing, concrete, fencing, gutters, landscaping, HVAC, and more)
  • Hot-lead flags for owner-builders and anyone hiring work out

Hot leads (owner-builders and hiring-out jobs) are listed first, then by project value.

Who it’s for

Local trade contractors

If you bid or take subcontract work in Sheridan and the surrounding area, this sheet is for you.
